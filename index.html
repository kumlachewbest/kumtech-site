01 / ABOUT

Engineering knowledge with real-world impact.

Kumlachew Yeneneh is an Associate Researcher and Lecturer at Ethiopian Defence University with a focus on Motor Vehicle Engineering, vehicle control, autonomous and semi-autonomous systems, alternative fuels and data-driven engineering.

He earned a B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Woldia University in 2018 and an M.Tech. in Motor Vehicle / Automotive Engineering in 2024. His work combines experimental engineering, mathematical modelling, intelligent control and machine learning to address practical transportation and energy challenges.
